Carry-forward lessons

01 · scanners are leads, not verdicts

An empty crypto_constant_scan or ioc_scan is not a clean bill — framework crypto, bespoke ciphers, and encoded stages leave no constants. Corroborate with imports + decompile.

02 · coverage ratio triages packers

High undefined-byte percentage plus a run-export (DllRegisterServer → regsvr32) equals a packed loader — a cheap, decisive call before any decompilation.

03 · behavior beats the label

Static blind triage nails what it does and how bad every time; exact family proper-names need OSINT / hash intel. Report the actionable call with high confidence, the label with humility.

04 · containment is the substrate

Every byte from Ghidra treated as inert under the untrusted-data envelope (ADR-005). No sample executed, no C2 contacted, worker killed on TTL — analysis stayed one-way.
